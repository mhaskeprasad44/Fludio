CAD, BIM & 3D Choreography

Week 2: Engineering Specs

Turnkey Responsibility Matrix

Scope of Work Division (Fludio vs. Project Civil Contractor)

Clear demarcation ensuring seamless MEP execution and zero on-site coordination clashes.

Project Work Package Fludio Engineering Scope Client / Civil Contractor Scope
Hydraulic & MEP Design Complete hydraulic loop sizing, flow calculations, CAD pipe layouts, and electrical load schedules. Provide site masterplan CAD, architectural constraints, and structural load approvals.
Civil Basin & Pump Room Provide detailed civil cut-outs, balancing tank sizing, sleeve penetrations, and puddle flange locations. Construct RCC pool basin, waterproof membrane, pump room/pit masonry, and tile finishes.
Equipment Manufacturing & Supply Manufacture 304L/316L nozzles, SS manifolds, IP68 LED lights, submersible pumps, and DMX control panels. Safe unloading and secure on-site storage area for equipment prior to erection.
Electrical & Water Utilities Provide electrical Single Line Diagram (SLD), cable schedule, panel termination, and low-voltage drivers. Provide incoming 3-phase raw power supply, earthing pit, fresh water refill line, and overflow drain.
Installation & Commissioning On-site mechanical erection, laser alignment of nozzles, DMX musical choreography programming, and handover. Provide clean water filling (potable/filtered) and access scaffolding if required.
