Turnkey Fountain Engineering & Installation Services

From preliminary architectural 3D visualization and hydraulic schematics to factory manifold fabrication, on-site commissioning, and comprehensive AMC maintenance across India.

Phase 01

Architectural Conceptualization & Hydraulic Modeling

We collaborate directly with architects, landscape consultants, and developers to engineer water features matched to site geometry, ambient wind conditions, and intended acoustics.

  • ✔ Fluid dynamic flow rate and head loss calculations (Darcy-Weisbach equations)
  • ✔ Detailed civil sump, trenching, overflow, and recirculation layout drawings
  • ✔ 3D simulation rendering nozzle plumes, lighting angles, and music cue timelines
Phase 02

Precision SS304L Fabrication & DMX Panel Assembly

Manufactured at our Narhe facility: custom circular/linear manifolds, CNC machined nozzles, IP68 underwater fixtures, and Schneider/Siemens electrical panels wired with Variable Frequency Drives.

  • ✔ Full hydrostatic pressure testing of stainless steel manifolds prior to dispatch
  • ✔ DMX 512 address mapping, VFD parameter programming, and audio track testing
  • ✔ Safe extra-low voltage (12V/24V DC) underwater lighting power distribution
Phase 03

On-Site Rigging, Piping & Musical Choreography

Fludio field engineers manage on-site mechanical alignment, manifold leveling, submersible pump installation, and acoustic choreography programming on location.

  • ✔ Submersible resin potting of all underwater wiring to IP68 standards
  • ✔ Fine-tuning water trajectory, nozzle heights, and spray column symmetry
  • ✔ Client team training on daily operation, timer scheduling, and backwashing
