10 What does the IP68 waterproof rating mean for fountain durability?

An IP68 certification signifies that the lighting fixture is 100% dust-tight and engineered for continuous, permanent underwater submersion at depths exceeding 1 meter (our units are pressure-tested up to 10 meters depth). 13 Why does Fludio standardize on SS304L stainless steel for all fountain hardware?

SS304L is an extra-low carbon variant of 304 austenitic stainless steel. The lower carbon content prevents chromium carbide precipitation during welding, virtually eliminating intergranular corrosion (weld decay) when exposed to chlorinated pool chemicals, sanitizers, and damp environments.

14 What stainless steel grade do you recommend for coastal areas like Mumbai or Goa?

For coastal installations located within 5 km of the sea (e.g., Marine Drive Mumbai, Juhu, or Goa resorts) where airborne chloride salinity is elevated, Fludio manufactures nozzles, manifolds, and lighting enclosures from marine-grade SS316 or SS316L, which contains 2–3% molybdenum for superior resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion.

16 How do interactive dry deck fountains work without an open pool of water?

Dry deck fountains feature a flush architectural walking surface made of heavy-duty 304L stainless steel grates or stone tiles. All nozzles, 24V solenoids, and flush IP68 lights are recessed in a subterranean drainage channel beneath the deck. Water shoots up through the grates and instantly drains back into the underground reservoir, leaving no standing water hazard.

19 How do floating lake aerators prevent foul odors and algae in ponds and lakes?

Floating aerators pull oxygen-depleted water from the lake bottom and spray it high into the air as a fine droplet plume. This transfers large volumes of atmospheric dissolved oxygen (DO) into the water and circulates the water body, which stimulates beneficial aerobic bacteria to digest organic sludge, eliminating foul sulfur odors and suffocating algae blooms.
