Laminar Sheet Physics & The Coanda Effect Over Waterfall Lips

An architectural sheetfall waterfall must produce a glassy, unbroken, translucent curtain of water that projects gracefully away from the feature wall. If internal turbulence is unmitigated, the water sheet ruptures prematurely into ragged spray strings, causing erratic splashing, aesthetic failure, and acoustic harshness.

Achieving a pristine sheer descent waterfall requires mastering the Coanda effect—the tendency of fluid jets to adhere to convex surfaces. Fludio precision-machines the waterfall discharge weir lip with a razor-sharp 45° downward chamfer and dual air-break relief grooves. This prevents water from clinging back along the wall cladding, ensuring clean release into the basin below.

Internal Flow Baffles & Hydraulic Calming Chambers

When pressurized water enters a weir trough from bottom or rear pipe inlets, it carries high localized momentum and chaotic eddy currents. To transform this turbulent influx into a placid, uniform sheet across weir spans up to 3000 mm, Fludio incorporates a proprietary dual-chamber internal baffle assembly.

The primary expansion chamber absorbs hydraulic kinetic energy and distributes flow across internal perforated diffuser plates. Water then cascades over an internal weir wall into the secondary calming chamber before spilling over the exterior lip at non-turbulent Reynolds numbers (( Re < 1500 )).

Flow Rate Sizing: Liters Per Minute Per Linear Meter

Proper pump sizing is paramount to waterfall sheet performance. If the flow rate is too low, the sheet narrows inward (necking) and ruptures halfway down. If flow is excessive, turbulent surging causes loud splashing.

For architectural sheer descents with vertical drops up to 1.5 meters, Fludio specifies:

Q_recommended = 90 to 140 LPM per linear meter of weir length

For monumental drops exceeding 2.5 meters or outdoor windy rooftop terraces, flow is increased to 160–220 LPM/meter with extended 150 mm projection weir lips.

Acoustic Tuning & Indoor Corporate Decibel Control

In corporate atriums, wellness retreats, and hotel lobbies, the acoustic signature of falling water must enhance relaxation without overpowering normal speech. Sound level is dictated by impact velocity:

V_impact = √(2g × H_drop)

By installing submerged acoustic deceleration baffles and pebble-covered anti-splash collection grates in the lower pool, decibel levels can be attenuated from an aggressive 75 dBA down to a soothing, biophilic 52 dBA white-noise background.

Technical FAQs & Operational Protocols

How do I prevent the waterfall sheet from breaking apart in outdoor installations?

Ensure the pump provides at least 120 LPM per linear meter, specify a 150 mm extended weir lip that throws water clear of the wall, and calibrate lower basin water level to absorb sheet momentum cleanly.

Can Fludio waterfall troughs be concealed within stone masonry or granite walls?

Yes. Fludio weir troughs feature compact low-profile rear chambers designed to fit directly within standard 200 mm cavity walls, brickwork, or dry-clad steel frames with only the stainless steel lip protruding.

What material is recommended for chlorinated swimming pool spillways?

For saline, salt-chlorinated, or high-bromine pools, Fludio custom-fabricates weir troughs using marine-grade AISI 316L stainless steel with full passivation and electropolishing.
