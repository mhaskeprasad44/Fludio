Laminar Flow Physics & Reynolds Number Stabilization

A laminar jet transforms water into a solid, glass-like rod of liquid that arcs smoothly through the air without turbulence, mist, or splash. When illuminated by an internal laser or LED, light travels inside the water stream via total internal reflection—identical to a flexible fiber optic cable.

Fluid mechanics dictates that laminar flow only exists when the Reynolds number (( Re )) is kept strictly below the critical threshold (( Re < 2100 )):

Re = (ρ × v × D) / μ

Fludio achieves this through a multi-stage deceleration chamber within the SS304L nozzle body. Water passes through layered micro-perforated stainless mesh discs and a high-density 150 mm polycarbonate hexagonal honeycomb matrix that dampens all radial vorticity, yielding perfectly uniform linear streamlines.

High-Speed Shutter Mechanics & Millisecond Water Slicing

The defining marvel of a 'Jumping Jet' is its ability to chop the continuous laminar stream into clean, flying segments of water—like liquid glass sausages—that jump across pathways from one collector basin to another.

This water slicing is executed by an ultra-fast precision mechanical shutter blade driven by a high-torque 24V DC rotary solenoid or voice-coil motor. Actuating in under 12 milliseconds, the razor-thin stainless steel blade cuts through the stream without creating droplet splash or disturbing the internal laminar cohesion of the severed water segment.

Total Internal Reflection & Integrated RGBW Optics

The optical brilliance of Fludio jumping laminar jets stems from Snell's Law and total internal reflection. Because water has a refractive index (( n approx 1.33 )) higher than ambient air (( n approx 1.00 )), light injected at the nozzle entrance is trapped within the liquid stream:

θ_critical = arcsin(1.00 / 1.33) ≈ 48.6°

Fludio houses a high-power 24W CREE RGBW LED chip directly behind a quartz optical window at the internal nozzle base. As the chopped water segment flies through the air, it glows intensely in vivid colors, releasing a brilliant burst of light only when it impacts the landing receptacle.

LiDAR, Time-of-Flight & Microwave Guest Sensors

In modern interactive splash plazas, theme parks, and science centers, jumping jets respond directly to guest movement. Fludio integrates industrial-grade IP67 solid-state LiDAR scanners and 24 GHz microwave radar sensors concealed in perimeter bollards.

When a pedestrian approaches, the PLC calculates their walking speed and triggers jumping jets to leap playfully directly over their head from left to right—creating unforgettable guest engagement with zero accidental wetting.

Technical FAQs & Operational Protocols

Can children play under the jumping laminar jets safely?

Yes. Laminar water arcs are completely smooth and cohesive with zero abrasive spray. Operating at low 24V DC SELV voltages, they comply fully with international splash pad safety standards.

What happens if wind blows on a jumping laminar jet?

Light breezes up to 10 km/h do not disrupt laminar cohesion. For gustier outdoor plazas, Fludio recommends keeping the throw distance under 4.0 meters or installing an automatic anemometer that pauses jumping sequences during high winds.

How fine does water filtration need to be for jumping laminar jets?

Extremely fine. A dual-stage filtration system with a 50-micron cyclonic pre-filter and a 5-micron polished cartridge filter is mandatory to keep the stream crystal-clear and protect the honeycomb rectifiers.
