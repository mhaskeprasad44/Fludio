Infinity Edge & Vanishing Spillway Hydraulic Engineering: Weir Flow Rates, Balancing Tank Sizing & Perimeter Slot Drains

Complete engineering guide to infinity pool hydraulics, Francis weir discharge equations, balancing surge tank volume calculations, and slot drain details.

Engineering Definition & Core Takeaway: Infinity vanishing-edge pool engineering relies on open-channel weir hydraulics governed by the Francis formula (Q = 2/3 * C_d * b * sqrt(2g) * H^(3/2)), where water continuously spills over a laser-leveled stone or acrylic crest into a subterranean catch trough. Sizing the secondary balancing (surge) tank requires calculating both the dynamic operational water sheet volume (crest height times surface area) and bather surge displacement to prevent weir starvation, cavitation in suction lines, or catastrophic perimeter pool overflow during high bather loads.

Open-Channel Hydraulics: The Francis Weir Discharge Formula

Creating a seamless visual illusion where water merges invisibly with the horizon or sky requires a continuous, glass-smooth water sheet over the vanishing crest. The required volumetric flow rate (Q) is determined using the Francis sharp-crested weir formula: Q = 2/3 × C_d × b × √(2g) × H^(3/2), where C_d is the discharge coefficient (typically 0.62 for stone crests), b is the spillway weir length in meters, g is gravitational acceleration (9.81 m/s²), and H is the hydraulic head height over the weir crest (typically 3 mm to 6 mm for quiet reflection pools, and 10 mm to 15 mm for visible cascading sheetfalls).

Key Engineering Takeaway: Precise Francis weir calculations ensure an unbroken, glass-like water sheet over the vanishing crest without excessive pump electrical sizing.

Balancing Surge Tank Sizing: Operational Sheet & Bather Surge

The single most common engineering failure in infinity pools is an undersized balancing (surge) tank, causing suction cavitation when pumps start or overflow when bathers enter. Fludio MEP engineers calculate total surge tank net volume (V_net) by summing three components: V_net = V_sheet + V_bathers + V_safety. V_sheet is the dynamic water volume suspended above the weir crest during active pumping (Pool Area × Head Height H). V_bathers accounts for water displaced by pool occupants (calculated at 0.075 m³ per person for 20% max pool capacity). V_safety provides reserve volume preventing dry-running during filter backwash.

Key Engineering Takeaway: Balancing tanks must accommodate both the dynamic hydraulic crest sheet volume and concurrent bather displacement to prevent cavitation or overflow.

Perimeter Slot Drains & Catch Trough Acoustic Design

Where the vanishing edge spills into the secondary catch trough, kinetic energy must be dissipated quietly without water splashing onto spectator decks. Fludio designs catchment troughs with 1:50 internal longitudinal slopes feeding into dual recessed sump suction boxes equipped with anti-vortex plates. To prevent hollow splashing echoes, the trough features inclined granite splash baffles or submerged perforated SS316L diffuser plates that absorb kinetic water impact silently.

Key Engineering Takeaway: Anti-vortex suction plates and inclined stone splash baffles eliminate hydraulic gurgling noise and suction cavitation in balancing lines.

Laser-Leveled Crest Tolerances & Precision SS316L Weir Edges

If an infinity edge weir deviates by just 2.0 mm across a 25-meter span, water will channel toward the lower side, leaving dry patches on the higher crest and completely shattering the vanishing-edge visual illusion. Fludio fabricates precision-machined AISI 316L stainless steel weir capping blades with adjustable micro-leveling jacking screws. Once anchored to concrete civil walls, blades are laser-leveled to within ±0.5 mm before being grouted with high-strength non-shrink epoxy mortar.

Key Engineering Takeaway: Adjustable stainless steel weir capping blades achieve ±0.5 mm laser-flat tolerances across 30+ meter spans, guaranteeing a 100% uniform water sheet.

Technical FAQs

Can an infinity pool spillway operate without a separate balancing surge tank?

No. If the catch trough is directly plumbed to pump suction without a balancing tank, starting the pump will instantly drain the trough before water circulates back, causing catastrophic pump cavitation and air-locking.

How do you prevent leaves and floating debris from clogging infinity edge trough drains?

Fludio installs continuous removable AISI 316L fine-mesh basket strainers inside the catch trough and dual multi-port suction boxes to trap debris before water enters balancing lines.

What material is best for the vanishing edge spillway crest?

Granite (flamed or honed), quartz, or Fludio's precision-machined electropolished SS316L capping blades offer the highest dimensional stability and resist biological algae adhesion.

How do you calculate evaporation losses from a large infinity pool?

Infinity edge pools evaporate approximately 25% to 40% more water than standard pools due to the high-surface-area falling water sheet. Fludio installs automated ultrasonic water level sensors with solenoid auto-fill valves.
