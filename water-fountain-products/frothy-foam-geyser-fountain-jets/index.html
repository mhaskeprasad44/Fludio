Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Is the Foam Geyser nozzle water-level dependent?

No. Unlike traditional Cascade or Schaumsprudler nozzles that require submergence up to a specific depth, Fludio Foam Geyser nozzles draw air through dedicated venturi ports and operate independently of water level fluctuations.

Why is a foam geyser preferred in windy outdoor locations?

The aerated foaming droplets are larger and denser than fine spray mists, meaning they fall directly back into the receiving pool even in 25+ km/h crosswinds, drastically reducing water topping-up costs.

What underwater lighting best illuminates frothy foam columns?

Because the water column is dense white and opaque, an IP68 center-hole Donut Ring Light mounted directly below the nozzle illuminates the plume internally from base to crown.
