Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Does the solar aerator work on cloudy or overcast days?

Yes. Our smart MPPT solar controllers initiate operation even at 20% solar irradiance, adjusting pump speed down to match available solar power rather than cutting out completely.

How does lake aeration eliminate foul odors and algae?

Dissolved oxygen oxidizes anaerobic bottom sediments (muck), starving blue-green algae of phosphorus while promoting beneficial aerobic bacteria that digest decaying organic matter.
