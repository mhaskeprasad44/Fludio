Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Why choose FRP fiberglass fountains over natural stone or marble?

Natural stone fountains weigh thousands of kilograms, absorb water, develop hairline cracks during weather shifts, and require expensive cranes to install. FRP fountains provide identical stone aesthetics at 75% less weight, with zero water seepage and decades of structural integrity.

Can FRP fountains be installed on rooftops and wooden decks?

Yes. Due to their lightweight composite structure, FRP fountains distribute minimal floor loads, making them the preferred choice for rooftop penthouses, clubhouses, and elevated terraces.

Does the color or stone texture fade in intense Indian sunlight?

No. Fludio uses UV-inhibited isophthalic gelcoats and automotive-grade non-fading pigments that withstand decades of harsh tropical sun, monsoon rain, and chlorinated water.
