Center-hole donut ring LED fixture engineered to mount directly over fountain nozzle supply pipes for 360-degree illuminated water columns.

Engineering Definition: An IP68 Donut Nozzle Submersible Ring Light is a circular underwater LED fixture featuring a hollow central conduit that slides directly around a fountain nozzle pipe. Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How do I choose the correct center hole size for my fountain ring light?

Measure the outside diameter of your nozzle pipe. Fludio manufactures 1" (32mm), 1.5" (48mm), 2" (60mm), and 2.5"/3" (76mm/90mm) center hole apertures to fit all standard BSP and NPT threaded pipes.

Can the donut ring light be synchronized with DMX 512 music shows?

Yes. Every Fludio ring light connects to our DMX 512 master control system, allowing synchronized strobing, smooth color fading, and instant scene changes matching the music beat.
