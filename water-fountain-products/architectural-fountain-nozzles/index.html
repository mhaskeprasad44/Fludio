Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

What is the difference between water-level dependent and independent nozzles?

Water-level dependent nozzles (like Cascade jets) draw water from the surrounding pool to generate their aerated plume; their height changes with pool water depth. Water-level independent nozzles (like Geyser jets) generate their foam effect purely from internal air injection chambers and perform consistently regardless of pool level changes.
