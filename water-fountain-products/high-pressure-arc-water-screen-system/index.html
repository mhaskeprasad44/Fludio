High-Pressure Arc Water Screen Projection Systems

★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 Rating • Verified Commercial Manufacturing

Fludio high-pressure arc water screen generators utilize high-thrust centrifugal pumps to project a 30-meter wide semicircular water mist screen into the air, creating a transparent, floating optical projection surface for 4K video and high-power lasers.

Engineering Definition: A Water Screen Projection System is an engineered multimedia water effect that ejects a continuous, ultra-fine fan of water into a vertical semicircular plane. The uniform water mist reflects and refracts projected light while maintaining transparency, creating the illusion of 3D holographic images floating over water.

What projector is needed to project video onto a water screen?

High-output laser projectors with 15,000 to 30,000+ ANSI lumens positioned for rear projection provide the highest contrast and vividness against the night sky.

Can the water screen operate in windy outdoor lakes?

Fludio water screen nozzles produce high-velocity laminar droplets that maintain a coherent optical screen in wind conditions up to 18 km/h.
