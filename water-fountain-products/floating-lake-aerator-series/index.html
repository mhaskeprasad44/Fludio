Floating Aerators Series

★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 Rating • 128 Verified Commercial Projects PAN India

High-efficiency floating lake aerators engineered for lake water oxygenation, ecological revitalization, and striking aesthetic water displays.

Engineering Definition: A floating lake aerator is a buoyant water-treatment machine featuring a continuous-duty IP68 submersible pump suspended beneath an HDPE float. It pulls stagnant, oxygen-depleted water from the lake bottom and sprays it into the atmosphere, transferring atmospheric dissolved oxygen, circulating the water body, and inhibiting blue-green algae growth.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How does a floating aerator improve water quality in lakes?

By continuously lifting bottom water to the air, floating aerators dramatically increase dissolved oxygen (DO) levels, allowing aerobic bacteria to digest organic sludge and nutrients, which clears cloudy water and prevents smelly anaerobic decomposition.

What power supply is needed for Fludio floating aerators?

2 HP models operate on standard 230V single-phase power, while 5 HP and 10 HP industrial models operate on 415V 3-phase power with integrated motor protection panels.
