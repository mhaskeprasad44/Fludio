Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Can a single pump operate this nozzle?

Yes. The internal manifold divides pump discharge evenly across all 19 to 61 individual orifices, allowing a single 1.5 HP to 5 HP submersible pump to drive the entire multi-tier feature.
