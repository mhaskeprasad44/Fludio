Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Why do large ring manifolds require multiple water inlet pipes?

If water enters a 6-meter ring from only one side, friction causes pressure to drop on the far side, resulting in uneven jet heights. Fludio designs ring manifolds with 2, 4, or 8 balanced bottom inlets to ensure uniform hydraulic pressure throughout.

Can the ring manifold be fabricated in sections for easier transportation?

Yes. Rings over 2.5 meters in diameter are manufactured in precision flanged interlocking segments with O-ring seals, allowing easy road transport and simple on-site bolted assembly.

What nozzles can be fitted to a Fludio ring manifold?

The welded female sockets accept standard Comet single jets, Frothy Cascade nozzles, or Fan jets. Each socket can also be fitted with our swivel ball joints for directional spray adjustment.
