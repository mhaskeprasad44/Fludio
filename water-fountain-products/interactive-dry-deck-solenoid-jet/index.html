Interactive Dry Deck Solenoid Jet

★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 Rating • 128 Verified Commercial Projects PAN India

Flush-to-grade dry deck fountain system featuring non-slip SS304L cover plates, integrated IP68 LED lights, and instant-action solenoid valves for interactive public plazas.

Engineering Definition: An interactive dry deck solenoid jet is a flush-floor architectural fountain module installed level with the surrounding pavement. Powered by high-speed IP68 submersible solenoid valves beneath a slip-resistant 304L stainless steel grate, it launches rapid vertical water pulses into the air that drain immediately through perimeter slots, leaving no standing water.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Yes. Fludio dry deck fountain systems incorporate industrial sand filtration, UV sterilizers, and automated chlorine/pH dosers to maintain drinking-water quality standards in compliance with public health regulations.

Yes. Fludio constructs our dry deck top grates from 5mm heavy-gauge 304L stainless steel reinforced with internal structural gussets, certified for service vehicles and pedestrian traffic.
