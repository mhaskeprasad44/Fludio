Architectural Wall Sheetfall Fountain

★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 Rating • 128 Verified Commercial Projects PAN India

Precision-engineered linear waterfall spillways creating smooth, glass-like sheets of descending water for luxury hospitality entrances, modern villas, and corporate lobbies.

Engineering Definition: An architectural sheetfall fountain (also called a water blade or spillway waterfall) is a precision-engineered 304L stainless steel manifold that channels pressurized water through an internal baffled dispersion chamber, causing it to exit as a uniform, continuous, transparent glass-like sheet of falling water.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How do I prevent the sheetfall water from breaking up before it hits the pool?

A water sheet stays unbroken up to approximately 1.5m to 2.0m fall height when properly sized with 75–90 LPM of flow per linear meter. In outdoor windy locations, positioning the spillway closer to the receiving basin preserves the glass-sheet effect.

Can Fludio custom-fabricate sheetfall spillways to fit specific wall curves or lengths?

Yes. In our Pune workshop, we custom-fabricate curved, corner, and extra-long 304L stainless steel spillways up to 4 meters in length with balanced internal manifolds.
