Interactive digital water curtain modules that dynamically write words, print company logos, and drop intricate geometric patterns through computerized falling water droplets.

Engineering Definition: A digital water curtain (or graphic interactive water waterfall) is a computer-controlled aquatic installation that uses dense arrays of high-speed micro-solenoid valves to release synchronized water droplets. Acting like a dot-matrix printer, falling droplets compose legible words, corporate logos, and animations in mid-air.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How do you program text and logos to appear in a digital water curtain?

Fludio's proprietary software imports standard graphics (PNG, JPG, BMP) or live keyboard text and converts pixels into on/off trigger commands for the 64 micro-solenoids across each meter, timing each drop so the graphic is crisp as it falls.

Can people walk through a digital water curtain without getting wet?

Yes. With optional ultrasonic or LiDAR motion sensors, the system can instantly stop water flow directly above a walking person, parting the waterfall like a doorway so they pass through dry.
