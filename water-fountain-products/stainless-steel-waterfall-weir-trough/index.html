Stainless Steel 304L Waterfall Weir Troughs & Spillways

★★★★★ 5.0 / 5.0 Rating • Verified Commercial Manufacturing

Manufactured from thick-gauge 304L/316L stainless steel, Fludio waterfall weir troughs feature internal multi-chamber honeycomb baffles that calm turbulent pump discharge, producing a continuous, glass-like laminar sheet of descending water.

Engineering Definition: A Waterfall Weir Trough is a precision-folded stainless steel basin that mounts inside stone, granite, or tiled masonry walls. It calms incoming pressurized water through internal diffusion baffles and discharges it over a laser-milled forward weir lip in an unbroken, transparent water blade.

Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

How do you calculate pump flow rate for a 1200mm waterfall trough?

A smooth, unbroken sheetfall requires approximately 60 to 90 liters per minute per linear meter of weir length. A 1200mm (1.2m) weir requires an effective flow of 4.5 to 6.5 m³/h at the spillway crest height.

Can LED lighting be integrated directly into the waterfall trough?

Yes. Fludio weir troughs feature an optional integrated stainless steel lighting channel along the underside of the lip that houses an IP68 silicone-encapsulated LED strip, illuminating the descending water sheet internally.
