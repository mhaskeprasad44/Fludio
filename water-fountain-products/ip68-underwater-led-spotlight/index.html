Frequently Asked Engineering Questions

Why is 304L stainless steel preferred for underwater fountain lights?

304L contains lower carbon content (less than 0.03%) than standard 304 steel, preventing carbide precipitation during welding. This delivers superior resistance to intergranular corrosion and chemical degradation in chlorinated fountain and pool water.

How does DMX 512 control work with Fludio underwater lights?

Fludio underwater lights integrate onboard or external DMX 512 decoders. Each fixture receives digital signal packets at 250 kbps, allowing each color channel (Red, Green, Blue, White) to be dimmed across 256 steps in synchronization with music.

Can these lights be installed in public swimming pools and interactive dry decks?

Yes. Operating at safe low voltage (12V or 24V DC), they fully comply with international IEC 60598-2-18 standards for underwater luminaires and human-contact public water installations.
